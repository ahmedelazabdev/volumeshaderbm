About the VolumeShader_BM Project

The VolumeShader_BM Project is an open-access hardware diagnostic initiative. Traditional GPU benchmarks evaluate polygon assembly and texture filtering, often masked by game-engine optimization tricks. VolumeShader_BM isolates the raw math processing speed of your GPU's fragment shader units.

Ray-Marching vs. Rasterization

Standard games place 3D triangles into a virtual coordinate space and rasterize them into pixels. VolumeShader_BM uses pure volumetric sphere-tracing:

  • No vertices or meshes are stored in RAM.
  • Rays are cast through camera coordinates using vector mathematics.
  • The 3D fractal boundary is evaluated dynamically with trigonometric series.

Transparent Hardware Assessment

All calculations occur inside your client browser through WebGL. Results are not warped by synthetic weighting curves or proprietary vendor adjustments.
