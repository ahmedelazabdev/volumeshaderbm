VolumeShader_BM: GPU Ray-Marching Stress Test & Diagnostic Guide

VolumeShader_BM (often abbreviated as VSBM) is a standardized web graphics benchmark engineered to test raw floating-point computing capabilities, shader processing units, and real-time thermal throttling across mobile and desktop GPUs.

Why is VolumeShader_BM Such an Intense Stress Test?

Traditional video game benchmarks evaluate rasterization pipelines: geometric vertex transformations, polygon counts, texture mapping units (TMUs), and raster operations (ROPs). In contrast, volumeshader_bm completely bypasses vertex geometry.

The entire visualization is generated through a full-screen quad driven by a custom GLSL fragment shader executing a volumetric ray-marching algorithm. Every single pixel on your display projects an independent ray through a virtual 3D space, iteratively evaluating the mathematical boundary of an 8th-order Mandelbulb fractal.

The Mandelbulb Equation

To approximate the distance of a point (x, y, z) to the fractal surface, the fragment shader repeatedly iterates spherical coordinates through the power equation: 

v' = v^8 + c
r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)
theta = atan(sqrt(x^2 + y^2), z)
phi = atan(y, x)
v^8 = r^8 * vec3(sin(theta*8)*cos(phi*8), sin(theta*8)*sin(phi*8), cos(theta*8))

Because this loop executes up to 64 iterations per ray step—accompanied by finite difference normal calculations for lighting—a single 1080p frame demands billions of trigonometric floating-point operations (FLOPs).

How to Detect Thermal Throttling

Observe the FPS counter during a continuous 2-minute run. If your device starts at 55–60 FPS and steadily deteriorates to 20–30 FPS, your mobile device or laptop chassis cannot dissipate the thermal load generated by the GPU's arithmetic logic units (ALUs), triggering hardware thermal throttling.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What causes the "Unsupported Canvas" / "不支持canvas浏览器" error?

This message occurs when your browser lacks access to an operational WebGL context. Common reasons include hardware acceleration being toggled off in browser settings, outdated GPU drivers, or running in an embedded in-app browser (such as TikTok or Instagram webview) that disables WebGL for security restrictions.

Can VolumeShader_BM damage my phone or computer?

No. Modern operating systems and GPU silicon feature integrated firmware safeguards (thermal cutoffs and dynamic voltage-frequency scaling) that throttle clock speeds automatically before dangerous temperatures are reached.

Why is the FPS capped at 60 FPS on high-end GPUs?

Browsers synchronize their requestAnimationFrame loop to the vertical refresh rate of your operating system (V-Sync). To test higher frames on desktop displays, set your monitor refresh rate to 120Hz, 144Hz, or 240Hz.
