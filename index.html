Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

1. Local Client-Side Execution

The VolumeShader_BM benchmark executes GLSL code entirely in your local browser sandbox. No benchmark scores, screen captures, or telemetry streams are sent to our servers.

2. Hardware Inspection Transparency

The tool queries the browser's WEBGL_debug_renderer_info extension purely to display your GPU model on screen. This string is processed in temporary browser memory and is never logged or transmitted.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics fingerprints, or external advertising beacons.
