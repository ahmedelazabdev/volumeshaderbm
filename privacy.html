Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

At VolumeShader_BM, accessible at your domain, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy documents the nature of data handled when you access and run our WebGL benchmark tools.

1. 100% Client-Side Computation

The core graphics benchmark operates entirely on your personal device within your web browser sandbox. The GLSL shaders are compiled by your device's GPU driver and executed locally:

  • No rendered frames, benchmarks, or graphic buffers are transmitted back to our servers.
  • Your compute operations remain private to your local hardware.

2. WebGL Telemetry and Hardware Queries

To present diagnostic feedback (such as displaying your GPU model and vendor), our scripts request standard browser WebGL extensions (e.g., WEBGL_debug_renderer_info). This parameter is read solely in runtime memory to display on your local interface. We do not log, harvest, or link this hardware identity string to your personal profile.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

VolumeShader_BM does not use marketing cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral fingerprinting. If you manually adjust test quality settings, these preferences may be stored in your browser's localStorage purely for usability convenience across page reloads.

4. Server Log Files

Like standard hosting infrastructure, our edge servers (CDN) automatically generate standard access logs. These records include Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser user-agent strings, referring URLs, and timestamps. These logs are maintained strictly for operational stability, network security, and DDoS prevention.

5. CCPA & GDPR Compliance

Because VolumeShader_BM does not harvest, store, or sell personal identifiers or biometric telemetry, our operations are fully aligned with the requirements of the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
